# TIV
THE INFORMED VOTER

Project meant to inform United States citizens of ongoing legislations in their county that they should be aware of! 

A non-partisan approach, meant to lay out the facts of new policies with a touch of AI summarization and cool retro style to make the process of becoming an informed voter just a bit less of a drag.
